PersonalOS

Delete your PersonalOS account and data

This page is for people who use PersonalOS, the app published by Sandeep Choudhary, and want their account and the data stored for it deleted.

In the app

  1. Open PersonalOS and go to Sync & account.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

If you can't use the app

Email sand.it.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my PersonalOS account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm from that address first, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, your name if you gave one, and your sign-in details.
  • Everything synced to our server: your encrypted data, the copies of your encryption key, and the list of your devices.
  • Your voice-entry usage counts and the record of your plan.

Nothing from a deleted account is kept afterwards, apart from what we are legally required to retain, if anything.

What is not deleted

  • The data on your phone. It stays in the app until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • A PersonalOS Plus subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions) so that you are not charged again.

Deleting only some of your data

You can delete any item inside the app; with sync on, the deletion reaches our server too. To have your synced data removed from our server while keeping your account, email us at the address above.
